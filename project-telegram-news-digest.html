TELEGRAM NEWS DIGEST

A daily news digest bot on Telegram with category buttons, fully automated.

Open live demo ↗
What it is

A Telegram bot that posts a news digest every morning at 7:00 AM IST. Inline buttons switch between India, World, Tech, Sports, Movies, AI News and Trending, each showing the top five stories. News comes from Google News categories and Google Trends.

The bot is on Telegram as @souravdigestbot. The source repository is private.

Highlights

Scheduled daily post through a cron job
Button taps handled by a webhook
Runs on a free plan
