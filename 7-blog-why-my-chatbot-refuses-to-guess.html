Why my admissions chatbot refuses to guess

NeotiaBot is an independent demo I built to see how far a retrieval chatbot can go on a free host. It answers admission questions for a university from a stored set of web pages and PDF brochures. It is not a client or university project.

The rule: answer from sources or say nothing

The bot has a 212-page snapshot of a website and 15 admission PDFs. Scanned brochures went through OCR once at build time, so nothing heavy runs when someone asks a question. All of the text is split into 125-word chunks that keep a link back to their source, about 2,950 chunks in total.

When a question comes in, the bot ranks those chunks and hands only the best evidence to Gemini. Gemini writes the answer from that evidence and cites the source and page. If the sources do not support the question, the bot says it cannot answer instead of making something up.

Why keyword search and not embeddings

The free hosting plan has 512 MB of memory. That ruled out embeddings and a vector database for me. So retrieval is lexical: keyword ranking with titles and topics boosted, plus aliases and topic-aware filtering. It is simpler, it fits the memory limit, and it still answers with sources.

A bug that taught me to test the refusals

At one point the bot refused a question about B.Tech Computer Science even though that information was in the index. Words like "offer" and "keep the answer short" were being read as part of the course name, so the match failed and the bot refused. I fixed the matching logic and added regression checks so that kind of refusal gets caught next time.

What it costs and what it does not do

It runs on a free host that sleeps when idle, so the first load can take about a minute. The public source snapshot has no credentials and no old history. It has a mobile app style interface, and you can try it at the live demo link below.
