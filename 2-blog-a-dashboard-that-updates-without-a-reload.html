A dashboard that updates without a reload

Academia is a learning management system I built from my own design screens. Students get a dashboard with their stats, homework, live-class reminders, a 360-question AI test bank, study hints, daily attendance and a streak. Admins manage students, courses, subjects, homework, materials, live classes, attendance, enquiries and feedback. The source repository is private, so this is a write-up of what I made and the details I cared about.

Small details that decide whether it feels real

A finished test now updates the dashboard without a page reload. It sounds minor, but a score that stays stale until you refresh makes the whole app feel broken.

Daily attendance has a clear rule with a midnight boundary in IST: a student has to use the app and finish four tests on the same IST day. The streak counts distinct days of use, not repeated requests, so refreshing a page cannot inflate it.

Ranks, certificates and approvals

Students earn XP and levels, and a rank ladder runs from Gold to Grandmaster with animated badges and auto-issued, named certificates. New students fill in their details and wait for admin approval before they get in.

Basic security I did not skip

Login is role-based for students and admins, with Google sign-in, email-code sign-up and password reset. I added rate limits, hashed passwords, security headers and a spam-protected public form.

Checking on the live app

Fixes were checked on the live app, including the mobile layout, not just on my own machine. The stack is Express, Prisma and Postgres on Supabase, with Vercel and Render for hosting.
