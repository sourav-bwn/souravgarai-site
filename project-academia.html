ACADEMIA LMS

A full learning platform: student dashboard, admin panel, AI tests, homework, live classes, XP ranks and certificates.

Open live demo ↗
What it is

A complete learning management system built from his own design screens. Students get a dashboard with real stats, homework, live-class reminders, a 360-question AI test bank, study hints, daily attendance and a streak. Admins manage students, courses, subjects, homework, materials, live classes, attendance, enquiries and feedback.

The source repository is private.

Highlights

Role-based login for students and admins, Google sign-in, email-code sign-up and password reset
New students fill their details and wait for admin approval
Ranks from Gold to Grandmaster with animated badges and auto-issued named certificates
Automatic attendance rule and daily streak
Rate limits, hashed passwords, security headers and a spam-protected public form
