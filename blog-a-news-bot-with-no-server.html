A news bot with no server

The Telegram News Digest posts a news digest every morning at 7:00 AM IST. Inline buttons switch between India, World, Tech, Sports, Movies, AI News and Trending, and each one shows the top five stories. News comes from Google News categories and Google Trends.

The constraint: no always-on server

Free hosting does not give me a process that stays running. So the bot is split into two parts that only run when they are needed. The schedule runs from a cron job that posts the digest, and button taps arrive by webhook, so a function wakes up, answers, and goes back to sleep.

Why I like this shape

There is nothing to keep alive and nothing to pay for. The bot runs on a free plan. The same idea shows up across my other projects: pick the free tier first, then design around what it cannot do.
